description: How a block stores items and connects to the conveyor network: the inventory component, its capacity, the shared conveyor definition and conveyor ports.
---
- InventoryComponent with its InventoryDefinition: the storage. It goes to the server only, where items live. MaxMass is the capacity in kg.
- ConveyorComponent: the connection to the conveyor network. Storage blocks share the definition AutogeneratedConveyorComponent; component_add(block, "ConveyorComponent", "AutogeneratedConveyorComponent") adds it.
- The network connects only at the block's port dummies: Type UseConveyor, a flat box facing out along its Z, with equal X and Y scale (the dummies page).
