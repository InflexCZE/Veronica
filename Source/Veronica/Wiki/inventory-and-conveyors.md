description: How a block stores items, is opened from the terminal and connects to the conveyor network: the inventory component, its capacity and tags, the shared conveyor definition, conveyor ports, and the explosion component vanilla containers carry.
---
- InventoryComponent with its InventoryDefinition: the storage. It goes to the server only, where items live. MaxMass is the capacity in kg. Vanilla cargo containers are in Assets/Blocks/CargoContainers/CargoContainers/<size in cm>, in sizes 150, 250 and 750 holding 16800, 67200 and 2150400 kg.
- Terminal: the terminal and the grid's inventory system find a block's inventories by type, so a block with an InventoryComponent shows its cargo in the terminal with nothing more added.
- ConveyorComponent: the connection to the conveyor network. Storage blocks share the definition AutogeneratedConveyorComponent; component_add(block, "ConveyorComponent", "AutogeneratedConveyorComponent") adds it. With it the network finds the block's inventories by their first tag, whatever its name.
- Ports: the network connects only at the block's port dummies, Type UseConveyor (the dummies page). A UseConveyor dummy is also where a player opens the block's inventory in the world; a block without conveyors uses a UseInventory dummy for that. Opening the inventory there needs no ConveyorComponent.
  - A flat box facing out along its Z. Its X and Y scale must be equal; the block fails validation otherwise. Vanilla CargoContainer150's front port is scale (1.5, 1.5, 0.05); side ports are the same box turned to face out.
  - Its centre should sit on a cell edge along its facing axis, so it meets the port of the next block; validation notes one that does not.
- Tags: a few optional components find the inventory by the tag Inventory, such as InventoryBlockDestructionExplosionComponent. component_add gives the inventory that tag when such a component is added.
- InventoryBlockDestructionExplosionComponent, on the server of vanilla containers: the block explodes when destroyed with ammo inside, scaled by how much. It is not what spills the items: the inventory spills them by itself.
