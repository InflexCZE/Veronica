- InventoryComponent with its InventoryDefinition: the storage. It goes to the server only, where items live. MaxMass is the capacity in kg. Vanilla cargo containers are in Assets/Blocks/CargoContainers/CargoContainers/<size in cm>. Their capacities grow steeply with size, far beyond what the block's look suggests, so a capacity to compare with is read from that size's InventoryDefinition, not estimated.
- InventoryDefinition.WhitelistedItems limits which items go in; an empty one admits every item. A copied vanilla inventory keeps vanilla's whitelist, so an inventory for a new item is a new InventoryDefinition with an empty whitelist.
- Terminal: the terminal and the grid's inventory system find a block's inventories by type, so a block with an InventoryComponent shows its cargo in the terminal with nothing more added. A UseTerminal dummy is only a spot that opens the grid's control panel from the block.
- ConveyorComponent: the connection to the conveyor network. Storage blocks share the definition AutogeneratedConveyorComponent; component_add(block, "ConveyorComponent", "AutogeneratedConveyorComponent") adds it. With it the network finds the block's inventories by their first tag, whatever its name.
- ConveyorComponentDefinition.ConveyorSubgraph: the block's inner nodes and the connections between its ports and inventories. Null, as on AutogeneratedConveyorComponent, generates one node joining every port and inventory of the block.
- Ports: the network connects only at the block's port dummies, Type UseConveyor (the dummies page). A UseConveyor dummy is also where a player opens the block's inventory in the world; a block without conveyors uses a UseInventory dummy for that. Opening the inventory there needs no ConveyorComponent.
  - A flat box facing out along its Z. Its X and Y scale must be equal, or validation fails with "Conveyor ports can be Pipeable only if they are square in shape".
  - Side ports are the same box turned to face out; copy the Transform and Scale of a vanilla dummy on the same side.
  - Its centre should sit on a cell edge along its facing axis, so it meets the port of the next block; validation notes one that does not.
- Tags: a few optional components find the inventory by the tag Inventory, such as InventoryBlockDestructionExplosionComponent. component_add gives the inventory that tag when such a component is added.
- InventoryStockpileComponent with its InventoryStockpileDefinition moves items between the inventory and the conveyors: it pushes out while the fill ratio (0 to 1) is above FillRatioPush and pulls while it is below FillRatioPull; null turns either off. FillRatioPullMargin affects only pulling.
  - A large inventory with a high FillRatioPush rarely pushes and fills up; lowering FillRatioPush, not MaxMass, makes it empty into the ship's cargo.
- FilterComponent: a conveyor item filter (whitelist or blacklist, drain all), used by the vanilla ComponentFilterSegment block. No sorting component exists.
- The player's backpack is an InventoryDefinition in Assets/Characters/System; a bigger backpack is an override of its MaxMass. More than one character InventoryDefinition is there: read them to find the one in use.
- InventoryBlockDestructionExplosionComponent, on the server of vanilla containers: the block explodes when destroyed with ammo inside, scaled by how much. It is not what spills the items: the inventory spills them by itself.
- A dropped or spawned item shows its own model only when FloatingObjectPrefabsConfiguration.ItemPrefabs maps its ItemDefinition to a prefab with a FloatingObjectComponent; otherwise it gets the fallback prefab, a generic cube. The value is the prefab's _Server definition (a client one fails to load). A mod overrides the vanilla configuration (Assets/System/Configurations) rather than adding a second.
