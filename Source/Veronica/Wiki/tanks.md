Parts:
- ResourceContainerComponent, on the server only, with its ResourceContainerDefinition:
  - ResourceType: the gas, Hydrogen or Oxygen (Assets/System/ResourceTypes).
  - MaxCapacity: how much it holds, in litres for both gases.
  - MaxChargingRate and MaxDischargeRate: how fast it fills and empties, in litres per second. Filling must stay below emptying.
- ConveyorComponent with the shared AutogeneratedConveyorComponent definition. Gases travel only through conveyors (their resource type requires them), so a tank fills and supplies only through its conveyor ports: UseConveyor dummies (the inventory-and-conveyors and dummies pages). A tank with no port holds nothing.
- ResourceContainerBlockDestructionExplosionComponent, on the server: a tank destroyed with gas inside explodes with its definition's Explosion, scaled between the empty and full scalars by how full it is; an empty tank does not explode. It explodes at the block's ExplosionCentre dummy, or at the block's centre without one.
- InteractionStatModifierComponent: raises a stat of the player using the block, such as their suit's gas, from the tank's gas (vanilla uses it at a UseTank dummy); its Stats decide which stat and how fast.
- FunctionalBlockComponent as the block component: a tank draws no power.

Vanilla tanks: Assets/Blocks/Tanks/Hydrogen/<size> and Assets/Blocks/Tanks/Oxygen/<size>. Each has its own ResourceContainerDefinition, explosion and block menu family (hydrogen tanks and oxygen tanks are separate families).

The Tanks template: a block made from it gets its own ResourceContainerDefinition (hydrogen, with a capacity and rates next to nothing), its own explosion definition with no Explosion (the game reports it as invalid), its own FunctionalBlockDefinition with no BlockKind (no block menu place), an InteractionStatModifierDefinition with no Stats (no suit refill) and no dummies.
