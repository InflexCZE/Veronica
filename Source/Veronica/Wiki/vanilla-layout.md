Folders:
- Blocks: Assets/Blocks/<family>/<name>/<size in cm>. Thrusters: Assets/Blocks/Thrusters/<class>/<size in cm>; list_folder shows the sizes.
- Source files sit in the vanilla Assets folders beside their definitions, each next to its .meta file: models as .fbx (e.g. Assets/Blocks/Walls/Corner/250/Models/NonFractured), textures and icons as .png (e.g. Assets/Blocks/Antennas/Antennas/1250/Antenna1250_Icon.png). GameData/Vanilla/Content is compiled output, not usable as a source.
- Materials are apart, in Assets/BlockMaterials; decal atlases and trim sheets in Assets/BlockMaterials/Decals/Atlases and Decals/Trims (e.g. AtlasDecal_Trims03).
- G-Screen categories, subcategories, tiles and block kinds: Assets/UI/Screens/GScreen.
- The environment configuration (skybox and more): find_content("EnvironmentResourcesConfiguration") finds it and Vanilla's override of it.

A block has one definition per component. Which one holds a value:
- ThrusterDefinition: force and how the thruster behaves.
- ThrusterEffectsComponentDefinition: the flame.
- BlockEffectsComponentDefinition: sounds.
- PowerableBlockDefinition (or the block's other *BlockDefinition): name (UIData.Name), icon (UIData.Icon), G-Screen kind (BlockKind), power, build cost, mount points.
- The block's size on the grid (BoundingBox, in cells) is generated from its model, not set in the definition. DisplaySizeOverride changes only the size shown to players, not the block's size.
- BlockModelComponentDefinition: the models, the build stages (BuildProgressModels, generated by the Editor action Regenerate BuildProgressModels on that definition) and the dummies.

find_content with words of the thing and of the definition type finds a definition, e.g. "IonThruster100 ThrusterEffects".
