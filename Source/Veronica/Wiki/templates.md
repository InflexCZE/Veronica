---
find_template("<what it does>") lists the templates of a function; create_prefab(template, name, folder) makes a new placeable block from one.

- Breakable templates expect a FractureModel and a DamagedModel besides the Model; NonBreakable ones do not.
- create_prefab's reply lists every generated file with its definition type. File names differ per template, so files are found by their definition type, not by a name.
- The template's BlockKind makes the new block a size variant of the template's vanilla family, in that family's G-Screen place. Another place is a change of its own (the add-to-gscreen skill).
- Some templates, such as Lights (Point), have no BlockKind. The G-Screen lists a block only through its kind, so such a block cannot be picked by players until it gets one: a vanilla family's kind, or its own (add-to-gscreen).
- A thruster template (find_template("thrust")) brings a ThrusterComponent with its own ThrusterDefinition, a ThrusterEffectsComponent for the flame, one Thruster-type dummy, and a PowerableBlockComponent as the block component.
- A storage template (Cargo Containers) brings an InventoryComponent and a ConveyorComponent, the explosion component vanilla containers carry, and a FunctionalBlockComponent as the block component. It brings no conveyor port dummies: the dummies list is empty until ports are added.
- A point light template (Lights (Point)) brings a PointLightComponent on both sides with its own PointLightDefinition, a PointLightRenderComponent with its own PointLightRenderDefinition, and a PowerableBlockComponent as the block component. It brings no Light-type dummy: the dummies list is empty, and the block gives no light until one is added. A spot light template (Lights (Spot)) brings the same with SpotLightComponent, SpotLightDefinition and SpotLightRenderComponent, and has the vanilla spotlights' BlockKind.
- A gyroscope template (Gyroscopes) brings a GyroscopeComponent with its own GyroscopeDefinition, whose MaxTorque is 0, and a PowerableBlockComponent as the block component. It needs no dummy.
- An antenna template (Antennas) brings an AntennaComponent that keeps the template's shared AntennaDefinition (a vanilla file; a range of its own needs a copy), and a PowerableBlockComponent as the block component. It needs no dummy.
- A landing gear template (Landing Gear) brings the MagneticBlockComponent, a lock detector and a release detector (DummyShapedEntityDetectorComponents), the alignment check, the cooldown and a PowerableBlockComponent as the block component. It brings neither detector's dummy (LockArea, CooldownRange): the dummies list is empty, and the block fails when built until both are added.
- A solar panel template (Solar Panels) brings a SolarGeneratorComponent with its own SolarGeneratorDefinition, and a FunctionalBlockComponent as the block component. It brings no sun sensor dummies: the dummies list is empty, and the block makes no power until they are added.
- An ore detector template (Ore Detector) brings an OreDetectorComponent on both sides that keeps the template's shared OreDetectorComponentDefinition, its own PowerableBlockDefinition with power Amount 0, and no dummies.
- The Sound Block template brings its own PowerableBlockDefinition with power Amount 0 but no sound components: SoundBlockServerComponent and SoundBlockComponent are added (the block-sound skill). It has no BreakableBlockComponent.
- The Gravity Generators template brings a GravityGeneratorComponent with its own GravityGeneratorDefinition and a PowerableBlockComponent drawing no power; its blocks start with reach and strength 0, so a built block makes no gravity until a player sets them (the block-gravity skill).
- The Wind Turbine template brings a WindTurbineComponent on the template's shared WindTurbineDefinition, whose output and clearance probe geometry fit the vanilla turbine's model; a block of its own size needs its own copy (the block-wind-turbine skill). It has no spinning blades.
- A timer template (Timers) brings a TimerBlockServerComponent that keeps the template's shared TimerBlockDefinition, the toolbar it runs, its sounds and status glow parts, its own PowerableBlockDefinition with power Amount 0, and no dummies.
- A battery template (Battery) brings a ResourceContainerInventoryComponent with its own ResourceContainerInventoryDefinition, one power cell slot (an InventoryComponent tagged CellSlot0 holding one cell), and its own PowerableBlockDefinition with no BlockKind and a ConsumedResource vanilla batteries do not have. It brings no CellSlot dummy. The older Batteries template stores a plain amount, without cells.
- A gas tank template (Tanks) brings a ResourceContainerComponent with its own ResourceContainerDefinition (hydrogen, capacity and rates next to nothing), a ConveyorComponent, the explosion a full tank makes when destroyed (its own definition, with no Explosion), a FunctionalBlockComponent as the block component, no BlockKind and no dummies, so no conveyor ports.
- A reactor template (Reactors) brings an ItemToResourceConverterComponent with its own ItemToResourceConverterDefinition (no fuel, next to no power), a fuel InventoryComponent and an InventoryStockpileComponent with their own definitions (pulling nothing from the conveyors), a ConveyorComponent, the explosion of a reactor destroyed while working, a PowerableBlockComponent as the block component and no dummies, so no conveyor ports.
- A sensor template (Sensor) brings a SensorComponent on the server that keeps the template's shared SensorComponentDefinition, the two toolbars the field triggers, its own PowerableBlockDefinition with power Amount 0, and no dummies.
- A template's own values, such as its Recipe, icon and names, come from some vanilla block of its family. They say nothing about the new block: the Cargo Containers template's recipe is CargoContainer150's, whatever size the new block is.
- A block made from a template for another function has none of these parts; they are added with component_add (the block-components page).
