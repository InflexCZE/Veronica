What mining gives:
- VoxelMaterialGameDefinition links a VoxelMaterialDefinition to YieldType (the ore ItemDefinition mining gives), YieldAmountPerM and DrillTime. Vanilla: Assets/Environment/System/VoxelMaterialGameDefinitions.
- The ore item has no reference back to the voxel material; find_references or find_content on the item name finds the link.
- A new ore: a voxel material, a VoxelMaterialGameDefinition pointing it at the ore item, and the material added to the generators or biomes that should place it (below).

Ore in space:
- Asteroids are generated by VoxelGeneratorDefinitions. Each sector, cluster and ring has a generator of its own, reached through the sector generation data: ProceduralGenerationConfiguration's SectorVolumes -> volume definition -> its voxel generator.
- ProceduralGenerationConfiguration's DefaultVoxelGenerator is only the fallback: procedural asteroids outside configured sectors, and asteroids placed by hand. An ore added only to the default generator does not reach the sectors.
- Vanilla generators: find_content for VoxelGeneratorDefinition; they sit under Assets/Procedural/<version>/Sectors/<Sector> and Assets/Procedural/<version>/System/Prefabs (e.g. CombinedGenerator-VS2-2). Change the one the area really uses, with an override.
- Group Ore Deposits: DepositsCountCoefficient (more deposits; scales with the shape's size) and DepositSizeDenominator (higher = smaller deposits). Both default to 0; a generator with 0 spawns no deposits.
- A deposit too big for its voxel is skipped: keep an ore's MinSize small enough for the asteroids it should appear in.

Ore on planets:
- Set per PlanetBiomeDefinition: OreRules for the ground, BoulderOreRules for boulders. Vanilla biomes: Assets/Procedural/<version>/Planets/<Planet>/Biomes; water areas have biomes of their own (e.g. Verdure_UnderWaterBiome_VS2-3). Change one with an override.
- Each entry: Probability, a relative weight (rescaled when the entries do not sum to 1), and Ore: Material (the voxel material), MinSize and MaxSize (deposit radius in metres), MinBiomeDepth and MaxBiomeDepth (0 the biome's top, 1 its bottom; no effect for boulder ores).
- Keep every Probability above 0.

Voxel material looks:
- GrassMaterial (a GrassMaterialDefinition) set to none removes the material's grass.
- Voxel and grass materials have no alpha mask.

Planets:
- Shape: CompositePlanetShapeDefinition. Water: PlanetaryWaterLayersDefinition and WaterVisualProfileDefinition.
- Heightmap: PlanetGeneratorDefinition. Its Heightmap takes a .planetcubemap (PlanetCubemapAsset, 16-bit height), the current form. The older six-PNG-face form is still read; validation asks for the new format while Heightmap is empty. Vanilla example: Assets/Procedural/<version>/Planets/MarsLike/Maps/Heightmaps.
- TerrainDetailCubemapDefinition.TargetPlanetRadius is the radius its detail patterns were made for; on a planet of another radius they are scaled. It does not set the planet's size.
- "Image metadata is corrupted. Pixel format is unknown" on a heightmap or biome map face: the image has no PNG metadata (not generated yet, or not read as PNG). A wrong colour type or bit depth gives "Image format ... is not supported" instead.
