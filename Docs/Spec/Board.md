# Board

The assistant shows the modder what it means instead of only naming it: an item opened in the Editor, or a graph of how items connect.

- The assistant can select a content item in the Editor and open it in the Render View. Items that open their own editor or an external application are selected only.
- It can draw a graph of content items on the Board, a panel in the Editor's main area: the items as Content Browser tiles with short notes, arrows from the referencing item to the referenced one.
- An answer about how items connect comes with a graph beside the text.
- A click on a node selects the item; a double-click opens it.
- The last graph stays until the next one.
- Graphs stay small: they explain a relation, not a whole tree.

## Out of scope

- Graph history.
- Anything but content items as nodes.
