# Optional follow-up skills are lost in a skill chain

A skill plan pauses when the next skill is activated and resumes when that skill's plan is done. A follow-up that a procedure names outside its plan is still easy to lose: `create-block` step 3 says to activate `add-to-gscreen` once its plan is done, but no item names it, and by the time `create-block`'s plan ends the model has worked through `block-thruster` and its last item ("Show the block") says nothing of the G-Screen.

Missing: a way for an optional follow-up skill to reach the end of a chain, or a chain shape that does not need it (for example the last skill of a chain naming the common follow-ups). Where to start: `Source/Veronica/Skills/create-block.md` step 3 and the Plan section of `Docs/Tech/Skills.md`.
