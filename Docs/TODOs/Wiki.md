# Wiki content and research behaviour

**Exists:** the wiki, its researcher and subagents (`Docs/Tech/Wiki.md`). Pages hold knowledge taken out of the skills and facts checked in the game source: block components and definitions, overrides and copies, dummies, particle effects, the vanilla layout, templates, the G-Screen, thrusters (units, power), inventory and conveyors (capacities, opening in the world), and the authoring subjects beyond the block families: materials and composite textures, models, LODs and model limits, collision, mount points, placement, breakable blocks, armor blocks, doors, subparts, pistons and rotors, characters, skyboxes, voxels and ores, localization, validation messages, block stats and build cost, block audio, weapons, configurations, survival unlocks, encounters and the mod project. Eval runs show one direct question per need is enough and the skills are not leaned too far for the build tasks.
