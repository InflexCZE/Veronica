# Wiki content and research behaviour

**Exists:** the wiki, its researcher and subagents (`Docs/Tech/Wiki.md`). Pages hold knowledge taken out of the skills and facts checked in the game source: block components and definitions, overrides and copies, dummies, particle effects, the vanilla layout, templates, the G-Screen, thrusters (units, power), inventory and conveyors (capacities, opening in the world). Eval runs show one direct question per need is enough and the skills are not leaned too far for the build tasks.

**Missing:**
- Content beyond the skills: facts mined from the game source (art rules, mount points, LODs, texture and cubemap naming, FMOD routing), and later the official SE2 modding guides on Steam rewritten without click-by-click walkthroughs.
